# Lowborn Promotions

This mod adds a new (hardened-compatible) feature to the training hall which
allows you to give one-time stat boosts to lowborn brothers by "promoting" them.

For all lowborn brothers above level 9, a new dialogue box will appear which
will let you pick a specialization for that brother for a one time fee of 3000
crowns.  The specializations are:

 - Sharpshot: 10 ranged skill.
 - Fighter: 5 melee skill and 5 melee defense.
 - Slayer: 10 melee skill.
 - Line Holder: 6 melee defense and 5 fatigue.

The specializations also make their daily wage go up to match what a highborn
would be paid.

The idea is to make the lowborn reach a similar effectiveness to a highborn
without having to dismiss them and train up a whole new bro.
